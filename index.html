How to read this

            
    
              
  • txID — SHA-256 of the protobuf-encoded raw_data. Fingerprint of what the key signed.
    • 
              
  • Decoded fields — ref block, timestamps, contract, owner, recipient, amount, fee_limit.
    • 
              
  • Signer — recovered from the signature when present, and checked against the owner address.
